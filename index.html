The problem

You already recorded a good episode. Then the subtitle work starts.

You export captions from CapCut or Whisper and they come out messy — so you fix them by hand. Every week.

What an auto-caption export actually looks like

  • um, uh, you know, like on every other line
  • The same word twice: so, so, I, I think
  • Lines that run too long, split in the wrong place, or repeat
  • Timestamps you still have to write by hand for show notes and YouTube chapters

Close to 1 hour of cleanup per episode

Scope

What it does — and what it does not do

One narrow job, done offline. If you need transcription or video editing, this is not that tool.

What it does

  • Cleans SRT and VTT files you already have — removes fillers like um, uh, you know, like, so
  • Removes repeated words and false starts — I, I think → I think
  • Fixes long lines and bad line breaks — max 37 characters per line, max 2 lines per caption, the Premiere Pro readability rule
  • Merges split lines and splits run-on lines, re-numbers captions, removes exact duplicates
  • Keeps your original timestamps — no re-timing
  • Lets you mark chapter points and exports a YouTube-ready chapters.txt
  • Exports clean SRT and VTT, plus MP3 chapters for your podcast file
  • Works offline on Windows and Mac — no account, no upload, no internet needed
  • Shows a diff preview first, so you can check every change

What it does NOT do

  • No transcription. You bring your own SRT or VTT. Don't have one yet? The FAQ below shows how to export one from CapCut or YouTube in 3 steps.
  • No AI show-notes generation. It does not write your summary, description, or episode notes.
  • No video editing or burned-in captions. It does not edit video, add captions to video, or export a captioned video file.
  • No cloud upload. Your audio and subtitles are not uploaded anywhere. It runs on your computer.
  • No auto chapter-title generation in v1. You pick the chapter points and titles. Automatic titles are not in the first version.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

I don't have an SRT — how do I export one from CapCut / YouTube in 3 steps?

From CapCut — 3 steps

  1. Open your project, go to Text > Auto Captions, and generate captions for your video.
  2. Select the caption track and check your captions are correct.
  3. Export your subtitles as an SRT file, then drop that SRT into CleanSRT.

From YouTube — 3 steps

  1. Go to YouTube Studio > Subtitles, and open the video you want.
  2. Find the subtitle track you want to use, open its menu (⋮), and choose Download.
  3. Save the .srt file, then drop it into CleanSRT.

Note: exact CapCut menu names may change by version. If your version labels it “Export subtitles” or “Export captions,” that is the same step. YouTube Studio layout and menu labels also change over time.

Does it work with Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve?

Yes — if you export an SRT or VTT from them, CleanSRT can clean it. In Premiere Pro: Window > Captions, then export your captions as SRT. In DaVinci Resolve: export your subtitle track as SRT.

CleanSRT formats captions to the Premiere readability rule — max 37 characters per line, max 2 lines per caption — so the cleaned file is ready to bring back into Premiere or Resolve. (Premiere itself doesn't hard-block longer lines; this is the readability rule the app enforces.)

CleanSRT does not run inside Premiere or Resolve. It is a separate desktop app: you export SRT out, clean it, and import the clean SRT back in.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. CleanSRT works offline on your computer. Your audio is never uploaded, and your SRT/VTT is not sent to a server. There is no cloud account to create.

What if it breaks my timestamps?

It shouldn't — CleanSRT keeps your original timestamps and shows you a diff preview before you export anything. Your original file is never overwritten. You export a new clean file, and you can compare it line by line first. If a change looks wrong, don't accept it.

In: SRT and VTT. Out: clean SRT, clean VTT, YouTube chapters.txt, and MP3 chapters.

Does CleanSRT transcribe my audio or video?

No. You bring your own SRT or VTT from CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, YouTube, or Whisper. CleanSRT cleans the text and timing you already have — it does not listen to your audio and create captions from scratch.

Windows and Mac, as a desktop app, offline. No browser version in v1.
